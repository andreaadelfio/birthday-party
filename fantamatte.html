The Secret Game

FantaMatte

Complete secret missions during the party to earn points and become the FantaMatte champion!

How to play

Disclaimer: Matte doesn't know this game exists so don't spoil it for him!

Rules: You need to convince Matte to do these tasks, and if Matte completes the task, you collect a certain amount of points. The more tasks you make him do, the more points you collect!

Example: for the task “scream CEEOOOHH” you need to convince Matte to scream CEEOOH, but how can you do that? You could ask him to scream it but he can become suspicious, so instead just scream it yourself and see if he replies with a big CEEOOOHH! If he does, you get 10 points! If other people start screaming CEOOHHH with him, you get an additional 30 points, and more!

To make sure that you actually have succeeded in your tasks, take a picture or a video with at least a witness, so that we will have a testimony, and, at the end of the party, a nice album!

Every time you succeed, just upload these pictures or videos here on this page, with the number that represents the ID of the task you completed. You can also write a simple note to tell what Matte did, or how crazy and amazing it was!

Bonus: if you manage, you can have him do more than one action at the same time. In this case, you will get 1.5x the sum of the scores of each successful action! And! If you discover that somebody else already asked him to do that same task, but you manage to convince him to do it again, you get 1.5x the score of the action!

All fun and games but how do you win? As the night goes on and more tasks are being completed, you will see your total scores on the LeaderBoard below, and also the pictures will be shown! The highest scorer will be the winner!

The prize? Of course, a nice hug from Matte on the stage! … haha just kidding, ETERNAL GLORY, that’s what awaits the friend who wins the FantaMatte!
