The Secret Game

FantaMatte

Complete secret missions during the party to earn points and become the FantaMatte Champion!

How to play

The first rule of Fanta Matte is: you do not talk about Fanta Matte.

Disclaimer: Matte has absolutely no idea this game exists. Don't spoil it for him! And most importantly, whatever you do, don't let him catch you using this website. Put your phone away and enjoy the party!

Rules: Your goal is to convince Matte to complete as many tasks as possible. Every time he completes one of your chosen tasks, you earn points. The more tasks you get him to complete, the more points you score!

Example: take the mission "Scream CEEOOOHH!".

You could simply ask him to scream it... but that might make him suspicious. A better strategy? Scream "CEEOOOHH!" yourself and see if he instinctively screams it back. If he does, congratulations—you've just earned 10 points!

Even better, multiple missions can be completed at the same time. Imagine everyone around him joins in and starts screaming CEEOOOHH as well. If that also completes another mission, you'll score points for both!

Claim your points: to prove that you've successfully completed a mission, take a photo or record a short video (around 30 seconds) showing Matte completing the task. Make sure there's at least one witness so everyone agrees the mission was actually completed.

At the end of the party we'll also have a fantastic album full of the night's funniest moments.

Every time you succeed, just upload these pictures or videos using the uploader below, together with the Mission # of the task that Matte completed. You can also write a simple note describing what happened and why it was so epic!

Combo Bonus: if you manage, you can have him do more than one action at the same time. In this case, you will get 1.2x the sum of the scores of each successful action! Simply write your tasks Ids separated by a comma ",".

Repeat Bonus: if you discover that somebody else (or even you) already asked him to do that same task, try to convince him to complete it again! If he does, you get 1.5x the score of that task!

All fun and games, but how do you win? As the night goes on and more tasks are being completed and proofs are being uploaded, you will see your total scores on the Leaderboard below, and also the pictures will be shown! The highest scorer will be the winner!

The prize? Of course, a nice hug from Matte on the stage!

… haha just kidding, ETERNAL GLORY, that’s what awaits the friend who wins the FantaMatte!
