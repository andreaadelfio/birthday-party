The Secret Game

FantaMatte

Complete secret missions during the party to earn points and become the FantaMatte Champion!

How to play

Disclaimer: Matte doesn't know this game exists, so don't spoil it for him!

Rules: You need to convince Matte to do these tasks, and if Matte completes the tasks of your choice, you collect points: the more tasks he completes for you, the more points you collect!

Example: for the task “scream CEEOOOHH” you need to convince Matte to scream CEEOOOHH, and how would you do that? You could simply ask him to scream it, but he can become suspicious. Instead, just scream it yourself and see if he replies with a big CEEOOOHH! If he does, you get 10 points! And other missions apply too, so for example, if other people start screaming CEOOHHH with him (that's the other mission), you get an additional 30 points, and more!

Claim your points: to prove that you have actually succeeded in making Matteo complete your task, take a picture or record a video (make it short, ~30 seconds) with at least a witness, so that we will have a testimony, and, at the end of the party, a nice album!

Every time you succeed, just upload these pictures or videos here in the uploader that you can find below, together with the Id number that represents the task that Matte completed for you. You can also write a simple note to tell what Matte did, and how crazy and amazing it was!

Bonus: if you manage, you can have him do more than one action at the same time. In this case, you will get 1.2x the sum of the scores of each successful action! Simply write your tasks Ids separated by a comma ",". And! If you discover that somebody else already asked him to do that same task, try to convince him to do it again! If he does, you get 1.5x the score of that task!

All fun and games, but how do you win? As the night goes on and more tasks are being completed and proofs are being uploaded, you will see your total scores on the Leaderboard below, and also the pictures will be shown! The highest scorer will be the winner!

The prize? Of course, a nice hug from Matte on the stage! … haha just kidding, ETERNAL GLORY, that’s what awaits the friend who wins the FantaMatte!
